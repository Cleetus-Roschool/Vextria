Why Vextria?

"At Vextria, our mission is to become a kid friendly, user-generated gaming platform for all ages to play, build and customize anything they possibly want."

Join the team at Vextria and help us in our mission. Vextria employees have built a global gaming cloud, a distributed physics engine, and a business with strong annual growth. We are looking for exceptional people to take our 3D game platform to the highest level of technological sophistication, and we continue to hire aggressively to support our growth and vision. We are motivated by significant opportunities to expand our audience, and to offer Vextria games on multiple platforms.

Please remember, at this time applying for a role is a voluntary role. You will not be getting paid Real Life cash for this position.

Benefits of Working at Vextria

You will work with a fantastic team. Plus, you will also have these additional benefits:

We let you take your time (off).

Our vacation policy is… well, we don't have a vacation policy; we're all about responsibility. You're a grownup, we trust you to figure out the time you need to have off as well as get all your work done.

You'll have a piece of our future.

Vextria is a privately held company and offers incentive stock options to all of our full-time regular employees.
