Community Guidelines

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to the community guidelines, Theses guidelines are to make a community that feels safe and welcoming for everyone, and a palce where you can be yourself and make friends.

Strike System

At Vextira we have a simple strike system for dealing with Abuse Reports. When a player is reported a moderator checks what was going on at all times to make sure its a real offense. The real offenses is listed below. Once we've decided the player did something wrong by our standards then the following actions are taken.

  1. Reminder - Kicked out of their current game and asked to read the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
  2. Warning - Kicked out again of their game as a final warning to read the Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
  3. Chat Banned - User won't be kicked out but unable to chat to any users e.g Fourms, Livechats, comments, ingame etc for the set period "1 day, 7 days, 1 month"
  4. Temporary Bans - Kicked out and not let back in for the temporary ban days, Which are "1 Hour, 12 hours, 1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days, 31 days, 180 days, 365 days"
  5. Permanent Bans - Kicked out and not allowed to access Vextira on that account. "Account Deleted" some users might get "IP / Device ban" which will not allow them to make an account.

If a member has false reporting or threats of being reported Well, the moderators look at the entire chatlog from the server, and look to see if anyone was doing anything against the rules, Sending in a report does not mean that the person you reported is going to be automatically banned, It means that one of our moderators will look at what everyone was saying and decide the proper action. Accusing someone is not enough to get them banend and reproting people for silly things just waste our time as moderators. Spamming the report system is not tolerated and will result in action taken against you instead of the person you reported.
