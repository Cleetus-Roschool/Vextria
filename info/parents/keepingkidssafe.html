Chat modes & filtering & Guidelines

Vextria uses chat filtering and moderation tools to help prevent inappropriate langauge and personal information from being shared through chat.

There are two types of chat modes available to each member:

  1. Super Safe Chat: This is the preferred mode for parents looking for the ultimate in online safety. This option limits what members can say or see to a specific list of pre-approved words. When an under 13 user signs up with their correct birth date, they are automatically put in this mode. Parents may change/disable their child to the next mode via the account settings
  2. Safe Chat: This mode allows members to type their own messages when communicating with other members. However every message that is typed first goes through a filter that blocks inappropriate words and phrases. Vextria's adaptive filter also learns to decipher and ctach codes or other methods that members might use to bypass the system. Please note: although our filter is continuously being updated and improved, some inappropriate comments may get through. In the event that this happens, members have easy access to a “report abuse” feature.

Blocking & Reporting System

Vextira provides tools that allow players to block, mute or report other users. If a player is behaving inappropriately or breaking the Community Guidelines the player or content can be reported to Vextria for a review.

If you block a User they will NOT be able to view your profile or games you created, For a game you won't be able to see anything such as badges recently played. Users can still see your Avatar In-game and leaderboard in game.

Configure Your Child's Account

Parents can review and configure certain settings on their child's Vextria account. These settings can help parents understand how their child uses Vextria and manage available communication features.

Account settings can be changed at any time as the needs of the player change.

Parent Accounts

Parents can use their account to review available settings and manage features associated with their child's Vextria account.
