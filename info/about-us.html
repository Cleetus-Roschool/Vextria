About Us

What is Vextria

Vextria is a Free, Kid-friendly platform, online gaming platform built around the classic style of 2013s multiplayer games. Vextria takes inspiration from the look and feel of the 2013 era of loneline gaming, featuring simple interface, user-gernerated worlds, classic-style avatars and a community-focused experience. Players can build and play their own or other games, customise their characters, and meet new people within the community.

Our goal is to create a free place where players of all ages can imagine, build, explore and play together without losing the charm and simplicity of older online games.

Vextria is currently under heavy development and is in public alpha testing. We deeply value community feedback as we contiune developing Vextria into a platform that can excite the imagination of both the young and the young at the heart

Vextria is currently a Windows-only product. Other verisons on Iphones, Androids, Macs or any other downloading of the Vextria application outside of Relatable sources such as Vextria.co.uk is malicious software.
