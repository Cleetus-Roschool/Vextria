About Vextria

Vextria is a online gaming platformbuilt around the classic style of early 2010s multiplayer games. Vextria takes inspiration from the look and feel of the 2012-2014 era of roblox with simple interfaces, block-based worlds, classic-style avatars, user-created games, and a community-focused experience.

Our goal is to create a free place where players all of kids aged (4 - 150) can build, explore, play games, customise their charactersw, and meet oither players without losing the charm of older online gaming.

Our product remains under heavy development and is currently in public alhpa testing. We deeply value community feedback as we continue to make Vextira into a game to excite the imagination of the young and the young at heart.

For more information about Vextria, visit the News and Help sections.

A Classic-Inspired Experience

We believe Modern games have changed a lot.

Userfaces have become larger, systems have become more complicated, and sometimes the simple fun of opening a game and just playing gets lost somewhere along the way.

Our attempt is to capture some of that older feeling and what created that era memorable
