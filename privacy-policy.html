Vextria Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Vextria is a gaming platform which operates in the United Kingdom of Great Britain. This Privacy Policy explains how Vextria collects, uses, stores and protects information relating to people who use our website, games, applications and services.

1. Information We Collect

Vextria may collect information that you provide directly and information that is automatically generated when you use our services.

Information You Provide

When creating or using a Vextria account, you may provide:

  • Username
  • Password
  • Email Address
  • Phone Numbers
  • 2 Step access
  • Date of Birth
  • Account Preferences
  • Profile Information
  • Information included in support requests

We only request information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service or feature.

Information Collected Automatically

When you access Vextria, our system may automatically receive:

  • IP Address
  • Operating System & Device Type
  • Date & time of connection
  • Browser type
  • Language preferences
  • Error and Crash information
  • Connection information
  • General technical information

Some of these informations may be considered personal data under UK data-protection law.

2. Information About Your Use of Vextria

When you use Vextria, certain information may be associated with your account. This may include:

  • Login History
  • Games you visit and Create
  • Account activity
  • Avatar Information
  • In-game information
  • Public profile information

The following information is used to provide the service and maintain your accounts status.

3. User-Created Information

Vextria may store information that you choose to submit to the platform. This can include:

  • Game information (Names, Descriptions, Icons, Thumbnails, Gamepasses, Badges)
  • Profile Information
  • Messages (Inbox or Website Chat or Ingame chat or Forum Posts)
  • Uploaded Assets (Catalog items, Images, Models, Audios)
  • Other content associated with your account

Information that you deliberately make public may be visible to other users. You should avoid placing personal information in publicly visible content.

4. Why We Collect Information

Vextria may process personal information for purposes including:

  • Creating Accounts
  • Providing access to Vextria
  • Maintaining account sessions
  • Saving account information
  • Providing requested features
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining technical functionality
  • Diagnosing errors
  • Protecting the security of our systems
  • Preventing unauthorised access
  • Maintaining reliable servers
  • Meeting legal obligations

We will not collect/send your personal information to outside 3rd parties.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the UK GDPR applies, Vextria processes personal data using appropriate legal bases. These may include:

Contract: When processing is necessary to provide a service you have requested, such as maintaining your Vextria account.

Legitimate interests: When processing is reasonably necessary for purposes such as security, fraud prevention, maintaining our infrastructure, or improving the reliability of our services.

Consent: Where applicable law requires us to obtain your consent before processing certain information.

Legal obligation: Where processing is necessary to comply with a legal requirement.

6. Cookies

Vextria may use cookies and similar technologies. Essential cookies may be used to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Keep accounts secure
  • Remember necessary settings
  • Operate website features
  • Maintain session information

Where consent is legally required for non-essential cookies, Vextria will request consent before using them. You may be able to control cookies through your browser or device settings. Disabling essential cookies may prevent some parts of Vextria from functioning correctly.

7. IP Addresses

Vextria may record IP addresses when users connect to our services. IP addresses may be used for purposes such as:

  • Security
  • Preventing abuse of our infrastructure
  • Detecting suspicious activity
  • Diagnosing technical problems
  • Maintaining server logs
  • Protecting accounts

IP addresses may be retained for a reasonable period based on their purpose. Your IP address will NOT be shared with our staff members and will not always be saved and we might use your Current IP address.

8. Children's Privacy

Vextria will be used by children and young people. We recognise that children's personal information requires additional protection.

Vextria aims to minimise the collection of children's personal data and will not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. Where UK law requires additional protections for children, Vextria will take those requirements into account.

We also consider the principles of the UK's Age Appropriate Design Code where it applies to our services. Children should not provide unnecessary personal information through their Vextria profile, games, comments, messages, or other publicly accessible areas. Parents or guardians with privacy concerns regarding a child's information may contact Vextria.

9. Public Information

Some information may be publicly visible when you choose to publish it. For example, depending on the features available, other users may be able to see:

  • Your username
  • Your avatar
  • Your public profile
  • Games you publish
  • Public creations
  • Public statistics
  • Public comments

Public information should not be considered private personal information.

10. Private Account Information

Certain information is intended to remain associated with your account rather than being publicly displayed. This may include:

  • Email address
  • Password information
  • Date of birth
  • IP addresses
  • Login information
  • Account-security information

We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorised access to this information.

11. Passwords

Vextria should not store account passwords in readable plain text. Passwords should be protected using appropriate cryptographic hashing and security practices. Your password should remain confidential. Vextria staff should not need to ask you for your password.

12. How We Protect Personal Data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. These may include:

  • Access controls
  • Password hashing
  • Secure authentication
  • Server security
  • Encryption where appropriate
  • Monitoring for security incidents
  • Limiting access to personal data

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Who May Receive Personal Data

Vextria may use third-party service providers to operate its infrastructure. These providers may include services responsible for hosting, databases, server infrastructure, email delivery, security, error reporting, payment processing, and customer support.

Third parties should only receive information reasonably necessary for the service they provide. Vextria does not sell personal data.

14. Legal Disclosure

Vextria may disclose personal data where required by applicable law. This may include responding to valid legal requests, court orders, law-enforcement requests, or regulatory requirements.

15. International Data Transfers

Vextria is operated from the United Kingdom. Some companies providing services to Vextria may process information outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred internationally, Vextria will use appropriate safeguards where required by UK data-protection law.

16. Data Retention

Vextria does not keep personal information indefinitely without a reason. When information is no longer required, it may be deleted, anonymised, or securely disposed of. Backup copies may remain for a limited period.

17. Deleting Your Personal Information

You may request deletion of personal information associated with your Vextria account where applicable. Some information may need to be retained where required by law.

18. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your circumstances and where UK GDPR applies, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and data portability.

19. Making a Privacy Request

To request access, correction, deletion, or other action concerning your personal information, please contact us with enough information for us to process your request.

20. Complaints

If you have concerns about the way Vextria handles your personal information, please contact the Vextria Privacy Team first.

You may also have the right to complain to the UK's data-protection regulator:

Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)
United Kingdom

21. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy shows when it was most recently changed.
